# BABTEK Website Content Governance

## Corporate accuracy

BABTEK Core Nigeria Limited is currently founder-led and developing its operating structure. Do not imply that all divisions have staffed teams, permanent premises, established product operations or in-house delivery capacity.

## Service representation

Distinguish:
- Services currently performed directly by the founder/company.
- Services coordinated through independent specialist professionals.
- Services available through a confirmed partner arrangement.
- Planned services and future capabilities.

Regulated professional responsibilities and statutory submissions must be assigned to appropriately qualified and registered professionals.

## Project portfolio

Every project record must carry a verified status:
- Proposed
- Ongoing
- Completed
- On Hold
- Cancelled

Do not describe a concept or design proposal as a completed building. Confirm project scope, BABTEK's role, client permissions, contributor credits and image rights before publication.

## Technology products

Use clear lifecycle labels:
- Concept
- Planned
- In development
- Beta / pilot
- Released
- Retired

Do not advertise unreleased initiatives as commercially available.

## Corporate details

Verify CAC registration details and public address against authoritative company records before publishing. Contact channels should be tested before launch.

## Editorial approval

All public company claims, news, case studies, vacancies, partnership offers and product announcements require internal approval before publication.
